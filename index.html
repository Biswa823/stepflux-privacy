Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 30, 2026

Welcome to StepFlux. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google or Phone Number, we receive your name, email address, profile photo, and phone number.
  • Fitness & Activity Data: Step count, walking distance, walking duration, calories burned, and walking speed — collected via your device's built-in motion sensors and GPS.
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS coordinates during active walking sessions to track your route and calculate distance. Location is only accessed when you actively start a walking session.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and sensor availability for compatibility purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To track your steps, distance, and fitness activity accurately.
  • To calculate and credit SFX (StepFlux Coins) rewards based on your walking activity.
  • To maintain your wallet balance and transaction history securely.
  • To verify the authenticity of walking sessions and prevent fraud.
  • To display personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore & Authentication). We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Per-user data isolation via unique Firebase User IDs (UID).
  • Server-side validation of walk sessions to prevent tampering.
  • Root/jailbreak device detection to protect reward integrity.

4. Third-Party Services

StepFlux uses the following third-party services that may collect data as per their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication and cloud database. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — Advertising and monetization. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — Location and authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap — Map tiles for walking route display. OSM Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

We display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalized ads based on your interests. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

6. Location Data

StepFlux accesses your location only during active walking sessions that you manually start. We do not track your location in the background without your knowledge. Location data is used solely to:

  • Calculate walking distance accurately.
  • Display your walking route on the map.
  • Detect GPS spoofing and fraudulent activity.

You can revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

StepFlux is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email address below. Upon account deletion, all personal data, walk history, and wallet records will be permanently removed within 30 days.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising.
  • Revoke location and sensor permissions.
  • Request a copy of your stored data.
  • Delete your account at any time via the app's Profile section.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with the updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
